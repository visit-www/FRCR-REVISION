Note HT: Histopathologic Type

The predominant cancer is squamous cell carcinoma. The staging guidelines are applicable to all forms of epithelial carcinoma, including those arising from minor salivary glands. Other nonepithelial tumors—such as those of lymphoid tissue, soft tissue, bone, and cartilage (i.e., lymphoma and sarcoma)—are not included. Histologic confirmation of diagnosis is required.
