Data Security

Multi-layered protection for patients and professionals.

PII Guard

A dual-layer protection system actively scans all text before it leaves your browser. Client-side detection identifies 24 categories of patient-identifiable information (names, NHS numbers, dates of birth, postcodes, hospital numbers). Server-side middleware provides a second line of defence. Detected items can be individually redacted, removed, or dismissed with full audit logging.

Zero Patient Data Storage

RadInsights does not store, retain, or log patient data. AI features process clinical text transiently to generate feedback and are designed as documentation support tools. No patient information persists on our servers after the request is completed. Report text stays in your browser session only.

Secure Authentication

All accounts are protected with secure session management and encrypted credentials. OAuth integration with Google provides trusted identity verification. Role-based access controls separate admin and user capabilities. Session tokens are rotated and invalidated on logout.

Encryption in Transit

All data transmitted between your browser and RadInsights is encrypted using TLS 1.2+. API calls to AI services use encrypted channels with authenticated endpoints. No unencrypted patient-adjacent data ever traverses the network.

Audit Trail

Every PII detection event is logged with the action taken (redact, remove, dismiss, or override), the type of data detected, a timestamp, and the user identity. Match text is never stored in logs — only the category and count. This HIPAA-aligned approach ensures accountability without compromising privacy.

Payment Security

All payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. RadInsights never sees, stores, or processes card numbers or payment credentials. Subscription management uses Stripe Checkout and webhook-driven state management.

AI Transparency

How we use AI responsibly in clinical education.

AI Assists, Humans Decide All AI-generated content — from report reviews to algorithm trees — is presented as a clinical suggestion, never as a definitive diagnosis. The qualified radiologist always has the final word.
No Black Boxes Our AI prompts are designed by a consultant radiologist using established guidelines (RCR, ACR, NICE, AJCC). Generated content is structured, traceable, and editable — never opaque or unexplainable.
Rate-Limited by Design AI usage is rate-limited per user per day across all tiers. This prevents misuse, controls costs, and ensures the platform remains sustainable and available for everyone.
No Ambient Voice or Autonomous Actions RadInsights does not use ambient voice technology, screen monitoring, or autonomous diagnostic workflows. Every AI interaction is explicitly initiated by the user.
Education First AI features are designed to teach, not to replace. Report reviews include teaching points, differential diagnoses, and references — building clinical competence rather than dependence.
