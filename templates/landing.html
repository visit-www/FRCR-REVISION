RadInsights is the product of a radiologist's intersecting passions — in radiology, coding, and artificial intelligence. It leverages cutting-edge technology and the best minds in radiology to make radiology engaging and a lifelong pursuit. The platform is designed to improve the quality and confidence of your daily reporting, while embracing the philosophy that the best radiologists remain students for life.

Five Pillars

From case-based learning to AI-driven reporting and clinical advisory, every feature is built with the working radiologist in mind.

Case-Based Learning

A bridge connecting textbook knowledge and clinical practice. Each case is structured as FRCR 2B viva-style questions, enhanced with image stacks, article links, spaced-repetition Q&A, SBA and Viva self-assessment, note-taking, and Notion integration.

Oncology & TNM Staging

Oncology does not need to be intimidating. The TNM Essentials module is a one-stop resource for staging concepts. Over 30 dedicated calculators enable real-time staging with clear rationale and concise crash courses.

RadInsight Intelligence

Think of a humble and very competent Radiology Consultant colleague guiding you through every step of your workflow. A highly evolved LLM dedicated to radiology — it reviews reports, highlights strengths, suggests improvements, and delivers targeted teaching points. Complemented by structured templates and interactive decision-tree algorithms.

RadIQ

A specialised LLM with six powerful AI agents for the tasks expected of a radiology consultant: draft GP referral responses, handle patient complaints, generate Datix-style incident reports, justify radiographer protocol requests, advise on imaging protocols and contrast, and answer general clinical queries.

Knowledge Hub

A comprehensive hub with clinical calculators, contrast media protocols, adverse reaction management, and incidental finding tools. Guidelines and safety protocols organized for instant access during reporting.

Simple is Superb

Every aspect of the platform is designed to be intuitive, efficient, and user-friendly. All content is intelligently cross-linked, allowing seamless navigation. A powerful AI-driven search engine enables instant access to content and resources across the entire platform. AI-Powered Reporting

Beyond the Shorthand

Radiologist-led AI that transforms your clinical shorthand into high-quality, structured reports — ready for MDTs, patient records, and professional communication.

INPUT — Clinical Shorthand

RLL mass 4.2cm, spiculated margins, pleural contact. Mediastinal LNs — R paratracheal 1.5cm, subcarinal 1.2cm. No mets. Suggest biopsy.

OUTPUT — Neural Language Report

Findings: A 4.2 cm spiculated mass is identified in the right lower lobe with broad pleural contact, raising concern for primary bronchogenic carcinoma. Mediastinal lymphadenopathy is noted with a 1.5 cm right paratracheal node and a 1.2 cm subcarinal node, suspicious for nodal metastatic involvement. No distant metastatic disease is identified.

Impression: Right lower lobe mass with mediastinal lymphadenopathy. Recommend tissue sampling for histological diagnosis and PET-CT for staging.
