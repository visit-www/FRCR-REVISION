RadInsights was born at the intersection of a passion for radiology, coding, and artificial intelligence. It is a platform that leverages cutting-edge technology and the best minds in radiology to make radiology engaging and a lifelong pursuit. The platform is designed to improve the quality and confidence of your daily reporting, while embracing the philosophy that the best radiologists remain students for life.

Five Pillars

From case-based learning to AI-driven reporting and clinical advisory, every feature is built with the working radiologist in mind.

Case-Based Learning

Each case bridges textbook knowledge and clinical practice. Structured as FRCR 2B viva-style questions, enhanced with image stacks, article links, spaced-repetition Q&A, SBA and Viva self-assessment, note-taking, and Notion integration.

Oncology & TNM Staging

Oncology does not need to be intimidating. The TNM Essentials module is a one-stop resource for staging concepts. Over 30 dedicated calculators enable real-time staging with clear rationale and concise crash courses.

RadInsight Intelligence

Your AI reporting companion. Reviews reports like a skilled consultant, highlighting strengths, areas for improvement, and targeted teaching points. Complemented by structured templates and interactive decision-tree algorithms.

RadIQ Clinical Advisory

Six powerful AI tools in one: draft GP referral responses, handle patient complaints, generate Datix-style incident reports, justify radiographer protocol requests, advise on imaging protocols and contrast, and answer general clinical queries.

Clinical Resources

A comprehensive hub with clinical calculators, contrast media protocols, adverse reaction management, and incidental finding tools. Guidelines and safety protocols organized for instant access during reporting.

Simple is Superb

Every aspect of the platform is designed to be intuitive, efficient, and user-friendly. All content is intelligently cross-linked, allowing seamless navigation. A powerful AI-driven search engine enables instant access to content and resources across the entire platform. A dedicated section allows users to request specific content, ensuring the platform evolves in collaboration with its users.
