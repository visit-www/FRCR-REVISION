RadInsights was born at the intersection of a passion for radiology, coding, and artificial intelligence. It is a platform that leverages cutting-edge technology and the best minds in radiology to make radiology engaging and a lifelong pursuit. The platform is designed to improve the quality and confidence of your daily reporting, while embracing the philosophy that the best radiologists remain students for life.

Four Pillars

From case-based learning to AI-driven reporting, every feature is built with the working radiologist in mind.

Case-Based Learning

Each case bridges the gap between textbook knowledge and clinical practice. Structured as FRCR 2B viva-style questions, cases are crafted to be easy to read, understand, retain, and apply. Enhanced with image stacks, article links, note-taking, and Notion integration.

Oncology & TNM Staging

Oncology does not need to be intimidating. The TNM Essentials module is a one-stop resource for staging concepts. Over 30 dedicated calculators enable real-time staging with clear rationale and concise crash courses on TNM classification.

AI-Powered Reporting

RadInsights Intelligence reviews your report like a skilled consultant at your side. It highlights strengths, areas for improvement, and provides targeted teaching points. Complemented by an ever-growing library of structured templates and interactive decision-tree algorithms.

Clinical Resources

A comprehensive resource hub with clinical calculators, contrast media protocols, adverse reaction management, and incidental finding tools. Guidelines and safety protocols are organized for instant access during reporting.

Simple is Superb

Every aspect of the platform is designed to be intuitive, efficient, and user-friendly. All content is intelligently cross-linked, allowing seamless navigation. A powerful AI-driven search engine enables instant access to content and resources across the entire platform. A dedicated section allows users to request specific content, ensuring the platform evolves in collaboration with its users.
