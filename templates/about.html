Purpose & Aim

Bridging the gap between textbook knowledge and clinical confidence.

The FRCR examination represents a critical transition point in a radiologist's career — the moment where theoretical knowledge must crystallise into practical competence. FRCR Revision was created to support this transformation.

Unlike generic study platforms, this app understands that radiology learning is inherently visual, case-based, and iterative. Each feature — from the image viewer to the structured Q&A format — is designed to simulate the real diagnostic process: observe, analyse, conclude, discuss.

The goal is not merely to help candidates pass an exam, but to build the pattern recognition, systematic approach, and clinical reasoning that defines an excellent radiologist.

Case-Based Learning

Real radiological cases with images, diagnoses, and structured discussions to build pattern recognition.

Structured Q&A

Question-and-answer pairs that mirror viva format, preparing you for clinical discussions.

Personal Notes & Highlights

Create your own annotations, highlight key points, and build a personalised revision library.

Discussion Forums

Engage with peers, share insights, and learn from community discussions on each case.

External Integrations

Connect with Notion for extended notes and Anki for spaced repetition flashcards.

Design Philosophy

Visual identity that reflects the precision and clarity of expert diagnosis.

FRCR-Revision is built for radiologists progressing from structured learning to clinical authority. Its visual identity reflects this transition through deliberate restraint. The mark is based on the idea of electromagnetic field coherence: multiple influences resolving into a single point of clarity. Rather than illustrating radiology or education directly, the identity removes explanation in favour of inevitability. What remains is quiet structure, balance, and focus — qualities shared by effective diagnosis and expert clinical judgement. The result is a symbol that does not compete for attention, but earns trust through calm correctness.

  • Deliberate Restraint Every element serves a purpose. Visual noise is eliminated to let content breathe and focus emerge naturally.
  • Coherence Over Complexity Multiple concepts resolve into single, clear actions — like multiple findings leading to one diagnosis.
  • Quiet Structure Information hierarchy guides without demanding. Structure is felt, not announced.
  • Calm Correctness Trust is built through reliability and precision — the same qualities that define clinical excellence.
