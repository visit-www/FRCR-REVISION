About RadInsights

Born at the intersection of radiology, coding, artificial intelligence, and a relentless pursuit of excellence.

Our Vision

More than a platform — a companion for every radiologist's journey.

RadInsights is the product of a radiologist's intersecting passions — in radiology, coding, and artificial intelligence. It leverages the best minds in radiology alongside cutting-edge technology to create a platform that genuinely serves the profession. It is built to transform the way you work and the way you learn, grounded in the belief that the best radiologists remain students for life.

Whether you are navigating a complex case or revisiting a concept you last studied years ago, RadInsights clarifies, contextualises, and promotes active learning — even in the middle of a busy reporting session. It is designed so that learning is not a separate activity but an integral part of clinical work.

RadInsights began as a focused FRCR 2B viva preparation tool: a personal project to organise cases, practise structured answers, and build the pattern recognition needed for the exam. Over time it has grown into a comprehensive platform that supports trainees, consultants, and reporting radiologists across all stages of their careers.

The Five Pillars

Everything RadInsights offers is built around five core pillars.

Case-Based Learning

A bridge connecting textbook knowledge and clinical practice. Each case is designed to simulate real diagnostic encounters, building the pattern recognition that defines expert radiology practice.

  • Image stacks with full diagnostic details and structured discussions
  • Full-text article links for deeper reading
  • Personal note-taking and Notion integration for extended study
  • Discussion forums for peer learning and community insights
  • Q&A format modelled after FRCR 2B viva-style questions

Oncology & TNM Staging

Oncology does not need to be intimidating. TNM Essentials is a one-stop resource for staging concepts — bringing together everything a reporting radiologist needs to stage accurately and confidently.

  • 30+ dedicated calculators for real-time TNM staging across all major cancers
  • Staging rationale and crash courses for each disease site
  • Designed for busy consultants who need staging clarity at the workstation
  • Integrated with case-based learning for contextual oncology education

RadInsight Intelligence

The product of hours of intensive work — a highly evolved LLM dedicated to radiology workflow. Think of a humble and very competent Radiology Consultant colleague guiding you through every step of your reporting: strengths, areas for improvement, and targeted teaching points.

  • Next Seat Consultant Radiologist Tutor — an AI chat companion with contextual pills: teach me, find references, generate SBAs & Q&A
  • Reporting templates for structured copy-paste into PACS
  • Interactive decision-tree algorithms for systematic reporting
  • Type directly or dictate-and-paste from your PACS workstation

RadIQ

A specialised LLM with six powerful AI agents for the tasks expected of a radiology consultant — the non-reporting work that fills your day.

  • Draft GP referral responses and patient complaint replies
  • Generate Datix-style incident reports
  • Justify radiographer protocol requests
  • Advise on imaging protocols and contrast considerations
  • Answer general clinical queries from a radiology perspective

Knowledge Hub

A comprehensive hub of the clinical tools, guidelines, and reference material that radiologists reach for every day — organised for instant access during reporting.

  • Clinical calculators (e.g. adrenal washout, LI-RADS) and commonly used guidelines
  • Contrast media protocols and adverse reaction management
  • Radiology tools: scoring systems and incidental findings workflows
  • Anatomy snippets and radiology pearls for quick reference

Security & Privacy

Privacy-first architecture, built to protect patients and professionals.

RadInsights processes clinical text to support your reporting workflow but does not store or retain patient data. All AI features are designed as clinical documentation support tools — never as autonomous diagnostic devices.

PII Protection Active detection and blocking of identifiable patient data, aligned with HIPAA standards and Royal College of Radiologists (RCR) guidance. No patient data is stored or transmitted.
Regulatory Classification Clinical documentation support tool (Class I). Not an autonomous diagnostic device. No ambient voice technology. All outputs require human professional validation.
Authentication & Access Two-factor authentication, secure session management, and role-based access controls to protect your account and data.
AI-Driven Search Intelligent search across all content types — cases, algorithms, templates, tools, and pearls — without compromising patient privacy.

Design Philosophy

"Simple is Superb" — the principle behind every design decision.

RadInsights is built for radiologists progressing from structured learning to clinical authority. Its visual identity reflects this transition through deliberate restraint. Content is cross-linked, AI-driven search connects every resource, and users can request new content directly — because the best tools adapt to the people who use them. What remains is quiet structure, balance, and focus: qualities shared by effective diagnosis and expert clinical judgement.

  • Deliberate Restraint Every element serves a purpose. Visual noise is eliminated to let content breathe and focus emerge naturally.
  • Coherence Over Complexity Multiple concepts resolve into single, clear actions — like multiple findings leading to one diagnosis.
  • Quiet Structure Information hierarchy guides without demanding. Structure is felt, not announced.
  • Calm Correctness Trust is built through reliability and precision — the same qualities that define clinical excellence.
