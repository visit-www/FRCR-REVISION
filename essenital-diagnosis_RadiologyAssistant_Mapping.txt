
FRCR 2B – High-Yield Case Mapping to Radiology Assistant Articles
=================================================================

NOTE:
- Radiology Assistant articles are thematic; multiple diagnoses map to a single core article.
- This mapping reflects how candidates are expected to THINK in FRCR 2B viva (pattern-based, not encyclopedic).
- Article titles follow RadiologyAssistant.nl canonical naming.
