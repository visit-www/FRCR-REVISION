### Pricing Strategy Rationale

**Why £14.99/£29.99 and not lower:**

1. **Market anchor is £40-60.** RefineRad's real prices set the ceiling. Even at intro pricing (£20-30), our standard tier undercuts both.
2. **We deliver more value.** TNM staging, clinical tools, cross-linked learning, PII guard, mobile, forums — RefineRad has none of these. Underpricing signals "budget product."
3. **Professional audience.** FRCR exam fees alone are £500+. UK radiology registrars earn £40,000-70,000/year. £15/month is a fraction of one day's pay.
4. **AI costs are real.** At £9.99 with heavy AI users, margins are razor-thin. £14.99 gives 40%+ margin on average usage.
5. **Annual plans reward commitment.** £129.99/year = £10.83/month effective — the "psychological £9.99" is still achievable via annual.

**Why not higher (£20+):**

1. We're a new brand — need to prove value before matching established players
2. Indian market is price-sensitive (₹1,499 is already premium there)
3. Lower price accelerates word-of-mouth: "It's cheaper AND better than RefineRad"
4. The founding member strategy means early users get an even better deal, driving reviews/testimonials

## 7. Regulatory & Legal Requirements

### Classification: NOT a Medical Device

Based on MHRA guidance, RadInsights is **NOT classified as a medical device** because it:

- **Does not calculate patient-specific diagnostic outputs** (no risk scores for individual patients)
- **Does not recommend treatments or drug doses** for specific patients
- **Does not analyse medical images to suggest diagnoses** for patient care
- **Functions as an educational/reference tool** — presents information for clinician interpretation
- **AI features are documentation support** — reviewing report structure, not making clinical decisions

> **MHRA test:** "Is the software intended by its manufacturer to be used for a specific medical purpose on individual patients?" → **No.** RadInsights is for education, reference, and documentation support.

This classification means:
- No UKCA mark required
- No Clinical Safety Officer required
- No MHRA registration required
- No medical device conformity assessment required

**CRITICAL CAVEAT:** This classification holds ONLY if we:
1. Never claim the tool diagnoses, treats, or provides patient-specific clinical decisions
2. Always include "educational purposes only" disclaimers
3. Never market it as a diagnostic aid for individual patient care
4. Ensure AI outputs state they require human professional validation

Our current landing page does not effectively communicate the breadth and depth of RadInsights' features. RefineRad's landing page, despite offering fewer features, **feels** more powerful because of how it presents them:

- **Before/after demo** (shorthand → consultant report) — immediately shows value

- **One-line feature pitches** with compelling names ("The Administrative Shield", "E-Rad Shield", "Interruption Management")
- **Strong CTAs** with action-oriented language

We have **more features and more depth** but our landing page doesn't convey this. Fixing this is P0 for conversion.

### Landing Page Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — use live demos and before/after examples, not just feature lists
2. **Name your features** — give memorable branded names (like RefineRad's "Administrative Shield")
   - Smart Reporter → "RadInsight Intelligence"
   - Finalize Report → "Consultant Refinement Engine"
   - Rad Shield → "The Clinical Shield" (or keep Rad Shield)
   - TNM Suite → "TNM Command Centre"
3. **Segment your audience** — trainees and consultants have different pain points; address both
4. **Price transparently** — show pricing on the landing page, not hidden behind a click
5. **Create urgency** — "Founding Member pricing: only 147 spots remaining"
6. **Mobile-first** — the landing page itself must be excellent on mobile (RefineRad's isn't)
